      How voting works. No account. Your vote is tied to a cookie on this device, a bot check runs on every vote, and each network can only add a limited number of new voters a day. We don't collect names or emails. IP addresses are kept only as a scrambled hash, for one day. The bot check is Cloudflare Turnstile, covered by its privacy addendum. You can move your vote once an hour.

      How the numbers work. A platform's score is its share of all current votes. The arrows compare it with the count 24 hours ago. A snapshot is saved every hour.

      What we mean by slop. Low-effort AI content made for reach, like generated posts, fake photos, bot replies and faceless video channels. This poll counts what people see in their feeds. It doesn't measure the platforms directly.
